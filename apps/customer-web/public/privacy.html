Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the V MOTIV mobile app and website

V MOTIV Technologies ("V MOTIV", "we", "us") runs a platform to buy, sell, rent and service cars in India. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our mobile app and website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using V MOTIV you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account and contact details

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, from Google Sign-In when you create an account.
  • Your phone number, which we use to coordinate bookings, test drives and deliveries, and to send one-time verification codes.
  • Saved addresses for service pickup and delivery.

Vehicle and booking information

  • Details of cars you save, enquire about, test drive, sell, rent or book for service (make, model, registration number, kilometres driven, condition notes, photos).
  • Your requests, quotations, offers, estimates, invoices and the history of each booking.

Rental driver details

  • When you rent a car we collect your driving licence number, expiry date, date of birth, years of driving experience and photos of the front and back of your licence. We use these only to check that you are eligible and insured to drive the rental car.

Photos, camera and microphone

  • Camera / photos: only when you choose to take or upload a picture, for example your licence or your car's photos when you sell it.
  • Microphone: only during a voice call you start or accept with our team. We do not listen in the background.

Payments

  • Payments are processed by our payment partner, Cashfree Payments, on their secure page. We do not see or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password. We keep the amount, the date, the payment reference and whether it succeeded.

Notifications and device information

  • A push-notification token so we can send you updates about your bookings, offers and calls, if you allow notifications.
  • Basic technical information such as app version, device type and error reports, used to keep the app working.
  • A copy of the car listings you have viewed is saved on your phone so you can browse offline. It contains no personal data.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and to verify your identity.
  • To show you cars, handle enquiries, schedule test drives, value and buy your car, and arrange ownership transfer.
  • To process rentals and service bookings, including checking driver eligibility, taking payments and refunds, and recording the condition of the car at hand-over and return.
  • To send you service messages: confirmations, payment receipts, reminders and updates (by app notification, SMS and email).
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To improve the app. We do not use your information for third-party advertising and we do not show ads in the app.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes and meet legal, accounting and tax requirements. Financial and transaction records are kept for the period the law requires. When data is no longer needed we delete or anonymise it.

5. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and separate roles for staff. Payment details are handled by Cashfree and never stored by us. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account and phone safe.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask to see, correct or delete your information. See how to delete your account and data.
  • Permissions: you can turn the camera, microphone and notifications off at any time in your phone's settings. Some features, like taking a licence photo or a voice call, then won't work.
  • Marketing messages: you can change these in the app under Marketing preferences, or by contacting us. Service messages about your bookings will continue.
  • You may also have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We will respond to valid requests within a reasonable time.

7. Children

V MOTIV is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and to rent a car you must hold a valid driving licence. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email.
