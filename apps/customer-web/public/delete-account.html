Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your V MOTIV account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

In the app: open the V MOTIV app, go to your account's Privacy settings and choose Request account deletion.

Or by email:

  1. Send an email to support@vmotiv.in with the subject "Delete my V MOTIV account".
  2. Write from the email address you signed in with (your Google account), and include the phone number on the account.
  3. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We then delete your account and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, saved addresses and preferences).
  • Your wishlist, saved searches, enquiries and notification settings.
  • Driving-licence details and licence photos you gave for rentals, once any open booking is complete.
  • Photos and documents you uploaded for selling your car, unless a sale is still in progress.

What we may have to keep

We keep records the law requires us to hold, such as invoices, payment and refund records, rental agreements and vehicle ownership-transfer papers, for the legally required period. These are kept securely, used only for those purposes, and removed when the period ends. We cannot delete an account while a booking, payment or sale is still open; we will tell you what is pending.
