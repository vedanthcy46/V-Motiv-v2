# Google Play listing text (V MOTIV)

Written for search (ASO) and for people: the words people actually type, used naturally, with nothing promised that the app does not do.
Paste each block into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing. Limits are Google's: title 30, short description 80, full description 4000 characters.

**Check before publishing:** every claim below is something the app does today, but please confirm the ones that depend on your operations (marked ⚠). Search volumes were not researched here; the keyword list is a starting point to check in Play Console's search-term reports.

```
V MOTIV: Car Service & Cars
```

## Other listing settings

- **Category:** Auto & Vehicles. **Tags:** Car service, Used cars, Auto repair (pick the closest Play offers).
- **Contact:** support@vmotiv.in · website https://vmotiv.in · privacy policy https://vmotiv.in/privacy
- **Screenshots (strongest levers for installs):** order them Home, Car Service booking, Service tracking, Buy (a car page), Sell, Scrap, My Garage. Add a short caption line on each ("Book a service in minutes"). The 1024 × 500 feature graphic and 512 icon are in this folder.
- **Languages:** adding Hindi, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu listings (translated by a native speaker) usually widens reach in India more than any keyword change.
- **Ratings drive ranking:** the app now has "Rate V MOTIV" in Account and a polite, occasional prompt. Reply to every Play review.

## Things not to claim unless true ⚠

"Free pickup and drop", specific cities, any inspection-point count such as "200-point inspection" (the inspector checklist has fewer items, so confirm the claim is true first), delivery times, price guarantees, "India's best/No. 1". The text above says none of these.
