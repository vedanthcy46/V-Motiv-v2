# V MOTIV V2 — Inspection Checklist Field Specification

Template Key: `vehicle-standard`  
Current Version: `2` (Full 16-Section Comprehensive Evaluation)

## Section-by-Section Breakdown

### 1. Vehicle Identity & Documents
- `identity.registration`: Physical registration plate matches declared enquiry. [Mandatory, Requires Photo]
- `identity.vin`: 17-digit VIN/Chassis marking physically observed and compared against RC. [Mandatory, Requires Photo]
- `identity.engine_number`: Engine block stamped number verified where safely accessible. [Optional, ICE/Hybrid]
- `identity.odometer`: Observed odometer reading recorded in kilometers. [Mandatory, Requires Photo, Number Unit: km]
- `identity.rc_document`: Original RC or DigiLocker/mParivahan verified. [Mandatory]
- `identity.insurance`: Active insurance policy verified. [Mandatory]
- `identity.hypothecation`: Loan hypothecation / NOC status declared. [Mandatory]
- `identity.discrepancy_flag`: Absence of unexplained vehicle discrepancies. [Mandatory]

### 2. Exterior Panels & Paint
- Panels: Bonnet, Front Bumper, Rear Bumper, Roof, Left Doors, Right Doors, Boot Lid/Tailgate, Fenders & Quarter Panels.
- Observations: Repaint indications, panel gap symmetry, dent/scratch severity, body rust/corrosion.

### 3. Glass, Lamps & Mirrors
- Front Windscreen (chip/crack check), Rear Windscreen & defogger lines, Side Window OEM stamps.
- Headlights (low/high beam), Tail lamps, Turn indicators & hazards, Left/Right ORVM mirrors and power adjustment.

### 4. Structural & Underbody
- Front apron & A/B/C pillar spot welds intact.
- Longitudinal chassis rails deformation check.
- Floorpan rust and underbody corrosion.
- Absence of unauthorized cut-and-weld structural repairs.
- Flood immersion indicators (silt in dashboard brackets, seat rail rust, harness corrosion).

### 5. Interior & Cabin
- Front & rear seat upholstery wear and adjustment rails.
- Dashboard, center console, and trim panel condition.
- Roof headliner cleanliness and sagging.
- Seatbelts retract cleanly and latch securely on all seats.
- Carpet and boot floor condition.

### 6. Cabin Systems & Electrical
- AC cooling performance and temperature differential.
- Cabin heater and multi-speed blower.
- Infotainment, touchscreen response, and speaker channels.
- Power window switches and smooth ascent/descent.
- Central locking and remote key fob operation.
- Wiper blades, wiper motor, and washer spray nozzles.
- Horn audibility and steering pad switch.

### 7. Engine & Powertrain (Conditional: Petrol, Diesel, CNG, Hybrid)
- Cold start cranking duration and starter health.
- Idle stability and absence of hunting/misfire.
- Fluid leak check (engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, fuel lines).
- Exhaust smoke observation (black soot, blue oil burning, white coolant vapor).
- Abnormal mechanical noises (timing chain, tappets, water pump, alternator bearing).
- Radiator fin condition, hose firmness, and coolant reservoir level.
- Engine and gearbox rubber mounting condition.

### 8. Transmission & Drivetrain
- Manual clutch pedal bite point, smoothness, and slip check.
- Gear selector engagement into all gears including reverse.
- Automatic transmission creep, kickdown, and shift delay check.
- Driveshaft CV joint boots torn or grease leakage.

### 9. Suspension & Steering
- Shock absorber oil leakage and rebound dampening.
- Lower arm ball joints, stabilizer links, and rubber bushings wear.
- Steering rack center feel and play.
- Abnormal clunks or squeaks over road undulations.

### 10. Braking System
- Brake pedal firmness, travel reserve, and vacuum booster assist.
- Visible brake pad friction lining thickness.
- Disc rotor scoring, lip wear, and drum condition.
- Handbrake / electronic parking brake hold on incline.

### 11. Tyres & Wheels
- Tread depth and sidewall condition for Front-Left, Front-Right, Rear-Left, Rear-Right.
- Wheel rims / alloy wheels curb rash, bends, or cracks.
- Spare tyre, hydraulic jack, and lug wrench availability.

### 12. Electrical & Safety Warnings
- 12V auxiliary battery terminal corrosion and state.
- Instrument cluster warning lamps extinguish after start (Check Engine, Oil, Airbag, ABS).
- Alternator charge indicator operation.

### 13. Road Test Observations
- Performed: `YES` or `NO`.
- If `NO`: Mandatory selection of documented reason (Unsafe condition, Customer declined, Severe traffic/weather).
- If `YES`: Acceleration under load, straight-line braking stability, high-speed cabin vibration, steering pull.

### 14. EV & Hybrid Systems (Conditional: Electric, Hybrid)
- Charging port pins, weather seal, and flap locking.
- Portable charging cable and emergency adapter available.
- Cluster State-of-Charge (SoC) % and range estimate.
- High-voltage isolation and drive system warning indicators.

### 15. Defects Register
- Itemized entries with Category, Severity (`MINOR`, `MODERATE`, `MAJOR`, `CRITICAL`), Description, and Location.

### 16. Final Declaration
- Acknowledged declaration: "I confirm that the recorded observations reflect the checks actually performed, and that any untested items, exceptions and known discrepancies have been documented."
