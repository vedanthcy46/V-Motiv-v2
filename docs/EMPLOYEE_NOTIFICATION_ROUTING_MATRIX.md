# Employee notification routing matrix

Generated from the event catalog. Recipients are **resolved per event** from live data (assignment, queue ownership, branch, permission); a role name alone never reaches anyone. Every candidate is re-checked at delivery: active employee and user, the permission or role gate (assignees are exempt because the business gave them the work), and branch authorisation. The person who caused the event, and the requester of an approval, are excluded. Each person gets one notification per event.

Strategies: ASSIGNED_EMPLOYEE / INSPECTOR_ASSIGNEE = the named assignee. BRANCH_QUEUE_OWNER, FINANCE_CASE_OWNER, ADMIN_RESPONDER, AUTHORIZED_SUBSCRIBERS, ASSIGNED_TEAM = members of the work queue (`NotificationQueueMember`), owners first. DESIGNATED_APPROVER = queue members, else holders of the permission. When nobody is designated the system falls back, flagged in the notification metadata, to at most 8 permission holders in the event's branch (branch staff before platform administrators); critical admin events fall back to SUPER_ADMIN / SECURITY_ADMIN only.

## Escalation policies

| Key | Respects working hours | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| `SALES_LEAD_FIRST_RESPONSE` | yes | after 60 min → REPORTS_TO; after 240 min → QUEUE_OWNER |
| `SALES_LEAD_UNASSIGNED` | yes | after 30 min → QUEUE_OWNER; after 120 min → ADMIN_RESPONDER |
| `OPS_DISPATCH_SLA` | yes | after 30 min → QUEUE_OWNER; after 120 min → ADMIN_RESPONDER |
| `INSPECTOR_JOB_OVERDUE` | yes | after 60 min → QUEUE_OWNER |
| `RENTAL_FOLLOW_UP` | yes | after 120 min → REPORTS_TO; after 480 min → QUEUE_OWNER |
| `FINANCE_STANDARD` | yes | after 240 min → REPORTS_TO; after 1440 min → ADMIN_RESPONDER |
| `ADMIN_APPROVAL` | yes | after 480 min → ADMIN_RESPONDER |
| `CRITICAL_INCIDENT` | no (day and night) | after 10 min → REPORTS_TO; after 30 min → ADMIN_RESPONDER |
